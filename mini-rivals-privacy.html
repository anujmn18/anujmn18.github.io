Privacy Policy for Mini Rivals

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Mini Rivals ("the app", package air.com.kidsstories.minirivals) handles, why, and the choices you have. "We" means the developer of Mini Rivals as listed on Google Play.

Summary

  • Mini Rivals has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device only

The app saves a few small settings on your phone so the game works as expected: how many times the app has been opened, and the time until which "ad-free" mode stays on after you watch a rewarded video. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Wins shown on the menu are kept only while the app is open. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mini Rivals is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

DataExample
IP addressUsed to estimate the general (approximate) location of the device
Product interactionsApp launches, taps, ad and video views
Diagnostic informationApp and SDK performance, such as launch time and crashes
Device identifiersAndroid advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. It is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads on some phones). You will still see ads, but they will be less personalised.

Permissions

The app uses the Internet and network-state permissions only to load ads, and the advertising ID permission for the ads described above. The games themselves work offline.

Children

Mini Rivals is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google AdMob described above, the app does not share any data with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any personal data on our own servers, because the app does not send any to us. Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app. For data held by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
